## High-Level Pitch (15 Seconds)

> *"In a modern electrical power grid, catastrophic transformer failures lead to multi-million-dollar blackouts, substation fires, and prolonged downtime. Our ML service monitors Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) telemetry in real time to diagnose specific internal fault modes, forecast remaining useful life, compute multi-objective risk scores, and deliver explainable maintenance actions before an outage occurs."*

### Step 3: Open Telemetry & Diagnostic Detail Panel (20 Seconds)
1. **Click on Row #1 (`2_trans_939.csv`)**:
   - The right-hand detail panel immediately loads the complete diagnostic telemetry profile, visual gauges, and explainability synthesis.
   - Observe the three visual risk meters:
     - **Composite Risk Score:** `85.12` (Critical Zone)

### Step 4: Inspect Fault Mechanics & Root Cause Drivers (45 Seconds)
1. **Point to the FDD Fault Classification**:
   - **Predicted Fault:** `Class 4: High-Energy Electrical Arcing / Flashover`
   - **Model Confidence:** `99.98%` (Derived from our balanced HistGradientBoostingClassifier)
2. **Explain the Depleted RUL**:
   - At `417 cycles`, this asset is experiencing accelerated degradation compared to the 1,000+ cycle baseline of healthy transformers.
3. **Review Top 3 Risk Factors (XAI)**:
   - Walk through the explainability factors generated by our permutation-importance engine:
     1. *Predicted Class 4 arcing pattern (confidence 100.0%)*
     2. *Critically depleted remaining useful life (approx. 417 cycles)*
     3. *Elevated terminal Ethylene concentration ($C_2H_4 = 0.00873$)*
   - *Speaker note:* *"Notice that high Ethylene ($C_2H_4$) and Acetylene ($C_2H_2$) generation slopes are classic thermodynamic signatures of thermal arcing (>700°C). Our ML model captured these physics-based relationships directly from the raw multi-gas time series."*

### Step 6: Grid-Level Architecture & Team Integration (30 Seconds)
1. **Bridge to the Downstream Grid-Level Risk Engine**:
